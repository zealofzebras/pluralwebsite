More help ↗
Do I need SmartMike+ microphones?

No. Plural can use built-in, USB and wired microphones too. You can combine them with SmartMike+ mics or record without any SmartMike at all. Android may only allow one local input at a time; Bluetooth headset support depends on the device.

Can I still use my SmartMike+ mics?

Yes, on Windows and Android. Pair the mics in your device's Bluetooth settings, then connect them in Plural. Each mic gets its own track and controls. Plural is independent of Sabinetek.

Can several phones record together?

Yes. Open Plural on each device and connect them over Wi-Fi or paired Bluetooth. Approve the connection on the other device, then start and stop recording from one place. Files are collected after recording; Wi-Fi is best for larger video takes.

Can I use the recordings in my editor?

Yes. Choose WAV, M4A or MP3 for a separate file per mic, or MKA, MKV or MP4 for a recording with multiple tracks. Support for multiple audio tracks varies by editor.

Can I use my mics with OBS or calls?

On Windows, Plural can send live mic audio through an installed virtual audio cable to other apps. A virtual cable is a separate installation. You do not need one to record in Plural.
